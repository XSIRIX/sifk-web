# Surface brief: home (`/`, `/en`)

Scope: home page, visitor mode Persuade. Also the pattern source for every other page (services, team, about, contact, Cloody, legal).
Audience and job: a German company lead who arrives cold and must know in seconds what SIFK does (two lines of business), believe it has people on both sides, and request a first call.
Proof on hand: 13 people, 7 countries, 32 client and partner logos, the white model world (one hero scene with film, seven place models, harbour town, company site before and after, container ship). No case studies, quotes or result figures exist.
Constraints: facts only, no invented clients or numbers, AA contrast, keyboard, reduced motion, pause on anything that keeps moving. Every route, canonical, hreflang, JSON-LD block and anchor id that existed before stays exactly as it was.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Weißes Modell, fünf Bänder. The world of SIFK is a matte white scale model, and the only colour in it is the five ribbons of the woven logo. Wherever a ribbon runs, something is happening. It refuses the dark consulting template, the golden-hour stock photograph, the pattern-strip "Africa" look, and every tint, gradient and soft card.

OWN-WORLD: Plaster ground (#ecebe9) and white, ink type in Barlow Condensed (display, 700) and Barlow (reading). The five ribbon colours of the logo (ink, glut red-orange, gold, lime, laub green) are never accents: each one is a whole flat field that owns a chapter. Pictures are photographs of white card-and-plaster models with flat paper ribbons in exactly those colours, cut out and standing on a field, with only their own cast shadow. Corners are square. No gradients, no interface shadows, no rounded cards, no icons in tinted boxes, no small labels above headings. Buttons are square bands; their hover is a weft wipe.

STORY: The visitor reads both lines of business in the headline over the model, sees who trusts SIFK, reads the four-line statement stepping down like weave, walks through the Africa chapter, slides sideways through seven countries and the people in each, flips the company building from before to after, watches the logo weave itself from horizontal (Germany) and vertical (Africa) ribbons, counts the team, meets the Cloody ship, and lands on a full-colour band that is one link to the first call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed model scene on its pale studio ground: a German company site under the Bergstrasse castle on the left, an African harbour and savanna on the right, ribbons weaving between them. In the empty upper half: the two-line headline in ink at poster size, one sentence, the first-call band and one text link. A row of five coloured chapter tabs sits on the bottom edge. The scene drifts as a slow film with a pause control; on scroll it scales back while the next sheet slides over it.

FORM: Colour chapters as stacking sheets of paper: every section is a full flat field that pins and is covered by the next one, like sheets laid on a model-maker's table. Seed key 39813d0b. Motion grammar taken from the logo: horizontal movement is the weft (Germany), vertical movement is the warp (Africa). Headlines rise line by line (warp), lists and statement lines slide in from the left (weft), the country track runs sideways, the logo weaves under the scroll.

Signature interactions: the weave scene (the traced logo assembles from its ribbons, scrubbed by scroll), the country track (seven models on their fields, pinned and moved sideways), the energy switch (the same building before and after, flips once by itself, then belongs to the visitor).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Build path: code-led, no approved comp. The owner gave complete freedom on design and copy; the decision round was skipped on that instruction. Pictures were generated with halleyfy; prompts are embedded in each raster and listed in asset-library/08-maquette/PROMPTS.md.
