## Energy buyers search by law and norm, not by town

Business buyers look for the energy audit by its legal and technical names. Google autocomplete shows real, repeated searches for "energieaudit pflicht", "energieaudit pflicht für kmu", "energieaudit kosten", "energieaudit bafa", "energieaudit nach din en 16247-1", "wie oft muss ein energieaudit gemacht werden", "energieberatung nichtwohngebäude kosten", "bafa modul 2 nichtwohngebäude" and "sanierungsfahrplan nichtwohngebäude beispiel" ([Google autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=de&gl=de&q=energieaudit)). Plain business wording like "Energieberatung KMU" or "Energieberatung Gewerbe" barely extends at all. **So the energy page should lead with "Energieaudit nach DIN EN 16247-1" and "Energieaudit-Pflicht", with "Energieberatung für Unternehmen" as the umbrella.** The only published volume figures are for homeowner terms such as "energieberater", and two vendors disagree by a factor of three (10,600 versus 33,100 searches a month), so they are no guide ([seoagentur.de](https://www.seoagentur.de/google-ads-tipps-energieberater/); [neukunden-kontakte.de](https://www.neukunden-kontakte.de/energieberater)).

The law is the engine of demand, and it is changing. **Today (as of 17 Nov 2026), every company that is not a small or mid-sized firm must do a DIN EN 16247-1 audit at least every four years, with fines up to 50,000 €** ([EDL-G](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/edl-g/BJNR148310010.html)). A government bill, Drucksache 21/8027, would switch the trigger from company size to energy use: **more than 2.77 GWh a year, averaged over three years**. It would also raise the threshold for a full energy-management system from 7.5 to 23.6 GWh ([DQS](https://www.dqsglobal.com/de/entdecken/blog/enefg-novelle-2026-und-edlg-aenderungen)). The bill had its first reading on 24 September 2026 and a hearing on 5 October 2026 ([Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw39-de-energieeffizienzrichtlinie-1211306); [Bundestag hearing](https://www.bundestag.de/ausschuesse/a09_wirtschaft/wp21_a09_Anhoerungen/1217440-1217440)). **No final vote was found by 17 November 2026; re-check before publishing.** If it passes, energy-heavy mid-sized firms become obliged for the first time. Those are exactly SIFK's buyers, and searches like "energieaudit 2 77 gwh" already appear. Several ranking pages state a wrong "11 October 2026" deadline ([klamm.de](https://www.klamm.de/news/energieaudit-2026-der-11-oktober-ist-nicht-die-frist-fuer-deutsche-unternehmen-4N1313914.html)), so a correct, dated answer is a real trust edge.

Funding is the second hook, and one deadline is too close to chase. The BAFA programme for consulting on non-residential buildings (EBN) pays **50% of the fee; for an audit (Modul 1) up to 3,000 €** if energy costs exceed 10,000 € a year. Only companies *not* obliged to audit qualify, and the consultant must be on the dena expert list ([BAFA Modul 1](https://www.bafa.de/DE/Energie/Energieberatung/Nichtwohngebaeude_Anlagen_Systeme/Modul1_Energieaudit/modul1_energieaudit_node.html); [rwb-consulting](https://www.rwb-consulting.de/lexikon/energieaudit/)). The building-analysis module (Modul 2, DIN V 18599) is capped at 850, 2,500 or 4,000 € by floor area ([ib-brinkmann, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.ib-brinkmann.de/blog/bafa-energieberatung-nichtwohngebaeude-2026)). **The EBN guideline ends 31 December 2026, and no extension was found as of 17 November 2026** ([NRW.BANK](https://www.nrwbank.de/de/foerderung/foerderprodukte/60039/energieberatung-fuer-nichtwohngebaeude-anlagen-und-systeme-ebn.html)). A new page would not rank in six weeks. Mention EBN on the energy page with its date, and update it in January 2027. Other programmes worth naming, with dates: the new building-subsidy guideline for non-residential buildings (BEG NWG), in force since 21 July 2026 ([rwb-consulting](https://www.rwb-consulting.de/blog/energieberatung/beg-2026-nichtwohngebaeude-foerderung/)), and the EEW "Transformationsplan" module, which pays 40 to 60% up to 60,000 € ([VDI/VDE-IT information sheet](https://www.wettbewerb-energieeffizienz.de/WENEFF/Redaktion/DE/PDF-Anlagen-FW/PDF-Anlagen-Transf/informationsblatt-transformationsPLAN-rili24.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=23)).

## One shared service page hides both businesses from Google

### Each service line needs its own address

The single most important website change is structural. Google builds one title, one main heading and one main topic per URL, and "#" sections do not count as separate URLs ([Google: JavaScript SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics)). In the main expert survey of local ranking factors, **"a dedicated page for each service" is the number-one factor for normal local results** (an opinion survey of 47 experts, not Google data) ([Whitespark 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)). Two unrelated businesses with two different buyers cannot share one title without weakening both. The fix is small: keep /leistungen as a short overview with two equal cards (which honours equal weight for Africa and Energy), keep both frozen "#" anchors on it, and add **one energy page and one Africa page, each in German and English**, plus a German-only voucher page. Do not build pages that only swap a town name. Google's spam policy names "pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" as doorway abuse ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). One honest "Einsatzgebiet Rhein-Neckar" section on the energy page carries the town names safely.

**This touches the frozen layer.** You froze routes, sitemap, canonicals, hreflang and structured data on 12 November 2026 because the sitemap was already submitted to Google (`DESIGN.md:221`). ### The Business Profile is the biggest single gap

Without a Business Profile, SIFK cannot appear in the Maps box at all. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and prominence includes links and reviews ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)). In the expert survey, **the main category, closeness to the searcher, keywords in the business name and an address in the searched town are the top four Maps factors** ([Whitespark 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)). You must make one honest choice. **Show the address only if the office is staffed and can receive clients during the posted hours**, even by appointment; otherwise set it up as a service-area business with the address hidden ([Google: profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)). ### Reviews: ask in person, never by unsolicited e-mail

Reviews are Google's own named signal and the area that gained most weight in 2026 expert opinion. In a US survey, **47% of people will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews**, and 74% look for reviews from the last three months ([BrightLocal 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)). The local bar is lower: the Heidelberg Maps box is held with 21, 7 and 5 reviews. A sensible target is **10 to 20 genuine reviews with text within 6 to 12 months**, then at least one every month or two. German law limits how you ask. The Federal Court of Justice ruled in 2018 that a review request by e-mail is advertising and needs prior consent (BGH VI ZR 225/17) ([WBS Legal](https://www.wbs.legal/wettbewerbsrecht/bgh-bewertungsaufforderung-in-rechnung-ist-unerlaubte-werbung-23833/)), and a 2025 legal analysis says the usual existing-customer exception does not apply ([IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/anforderungen-bewertungsanfrage-mail.html)). So ask every client in person at the final meeting or on paper, and by e-mail only with separate, confirmed consent. Google forbids rewards, asking only happy clients, and reviews from staff ([Google review policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en)). Reply to every review within a few days, in the reviewer's language, without project secrets.

## Conclusion

The fastest visible win needs almost no code. The Maps box in this region goes to firms with a handful of reviews, and fachrat already calls SIFK the only checked energy adviser based in Schriesheim. A verified profile, a sign on the door and a polite ask at every project close may do more in three months than any website change. The structural fix is the opposite: it is cheap to build but needs one decision from you. The freeze protects the pages Google already knows, and adding pages does not move any of them, so the real question is whether you allow additions, not changes.

The two service lines also run on different clocks. Energy demand is about to be reset by law, so dated, accurate answers to "do I have to audit?" will age into lasting assets. The Africa line holds the rarer asset today, a place among about 55 BAFA-listed advisers, but that asset loses its search value on 28 February 2027. It should be used now and then turned into lasting pages built on the real advantage: people on the ground in Uganda, Kenya and Ethiopia. And because no reliable search-volume data exists for any of these terms, the first month of Search Console numbers should be treated as the real keyword study and used to re-order everything above.
